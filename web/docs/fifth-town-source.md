# Fifth-town source boundary

The fifth town uses the original ordinary shop sequence, five negative cards,
calendar-based mission preparation and a level-two or level-three recruit.
The existing search value and shared item-slot metadata survive the transition.
The sixth battle and hiring remain outside this boundary.

## Stock, mission and economy

`MainTimeline.as:3155` (`frame71`) enables the MOD sale only when `day % 10 == 4`.
Day5 therefore follows the normal `generateShop:5246` path: clear occupancy while
retaining slot fields, generate two default stock entries, then replace slot0
with the gun-mode offer. All preceding random draws remain part of generation.
The prior town's sale does not persist into the new stock. No extra card or
search-generation rule appears on this day.

`generateSpecialLoot:12064` consumes the optional Ragnarok roll before adding
Holy Grail1037 when `day % 8 == 5`. This belongs to the mission prepared in town5;
Day5's battle uses mission4 and its earlier Archer statue guarantee. Mission5
has power1400, reward700 and spawn rate14; its waves are not generated by town
preparation. Absorption is copied from the medals owned at preparation time,
without changing the active battle's historical parameters.

`calcTax:3587` gives `max(0, day * 35 - 200)` in normal mode, still0 on Day5.
`newTitle:9752` keeps title income0 through Day5. `calcUnkeep:1660` remains40 with
the current default castle/storage and no relevant badge. These statements
assume the supported solo/default-castle campaign; search, hiring and castle
upgrades would require their own economy changes. Day6 introduces different
tax/title conditions and is not implicitly enabled by this extension.

## Recruit growth and saves

`generateRecruit:7709` requests
`floor(random * day * 0.3) + floor(day / 5) + 1`. On Day5 this yields2 or3.
`generatePlayer:2075` invokes `levelUp(false):10480` once or twice before nextTrait,
portrait and class equipment. Each growth step spends two independent potential
rolls, increments the class rank and leaves one unspent skill point. Final SP
is1 or2, cost450 or500, and hidden aiming/teamwork can reach12 after two gains.
No skill-selection method is called during recruitment.

The existing class equipment assignments run after growth. Prior Militia slots
47/48 remain when the new class does not overwrite them. Validation binds the
candidate's level, SP, class rank, cost and hidden-growth bounds to its own town
calendar. In particular, previous town4 may contain a level-one recruit even
when the current town5 requires level2–3.

## Independent evidence

Two identical original Ruffle runs captured **17 observations**: six full towns
using seeds42/1/3735928559 with no medals or medals60/61, and eleven controlled
recruit probes. The full towns produced levels2/2/3 and consumed244/112/204 draws
respectively. The probes cover all nine classes at level3, reversed hidden-growth
outcomes and the level2 minimum. Controlled prefixes continue advancing the
underlying xorshift stream and are explicitly diagnostic inputs.

All17 match production exactly on the first comparison, including item metadata,
cards, traits, mission, RNG counts/state and the original save boundary. Five
focused tests include provenance, an extra-draw negative control and malformed
grown-recruit saves. Earlier town fixtures remain unchanged. See
[oracle provenance and reproduction](../../docs/oracle-notes.md) and
`tests/fixtures/original-fifth-town-trace.json`. Native menus, hiring and complete
campaign timing are separate checks.
