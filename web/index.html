Touch controls

Landscape only. Places a small, translucent Reload button closer to your thumb. Saved in this browser.

  • Tap or drag on the battlefield to shoot. Release to fire a bow.
  • Tap an item to use it.
  • Tap Reload to refill your weapon. Auto toggles automatic aim and fire.
  • Rotate your device for more room. In portrait menus, use Enlarge and View.
