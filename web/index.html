Touch controls

Landscape only. Moves the Reload button onto the battlefield, closer to your right thumb.

Tap a target to shoot. Hold and drag to aim an automatic weapon. With a bow, lift your finger to release the arrow.

Tap an item in the bottom row to use it. Tap Reload to refill your weapon or Fire at will to toggle automatic aim and fire.

Tap Reload just before your weapon is ready to queue it. Repeated taps keep it queued. A new shot, a weapon change or pausing clears the queue.

Turn your phone sideways for a larger playfield. In portrait, tap Enlarge in a menu and slide View to reach either side. Swipe the text in Help, Statistics and the day report to scroll.
