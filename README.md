# Elona Shooter Plus

**[Play in your browser](https://pastalapizza.site/elona/)**

An unofficial browser port of **Elona Shooter Plus 1.3.0.4**, the Flash castle-defense game. Pick a hero, aim at the monsters and keep your castle standing. Between battles, buy weapons, recruit companions and spend your earnings in town.

## Start playing

Choose **New Game**, pick a class and appearance, then press **Proceed**. Open **Help** from the title or pause screen for the original instructions.

- **Desktop:** aim and shoot with the mouse. Hold and release for bows. Space reloads, 1–5 select weapons or use items, F toggles automatic fire, and Esc pauses.
- **Touch:** tap to shoot, or hold and drag to aim an automatic weapon. A second finger can tap Reload. Landscape gives the battlefield more room; portrait menus have an Enlarge control.

Use **EN / RU** beside the mute and fullscreen buttons to switch languages at any time. The choice is remembered. Russian includes the lettering in the original menu artwork, and works with both editions and existing saves.

## Classic or Expanded?

**Classic** keeps the original content with gameplay fixes. **Expanded** adds optional fan-made features, starting with a companion targeting order. Choose before starting an adventure; loading a save keeps its edition. [Edition details](docs/expanded-edition.md).

## Keep your saves

Saves stay in your browser, not an account. Both editions use the same eight labeled slots, but have separate medal collections. Choose an empty slot to keep another adventure. Export a JSON backup before clearing browser data or moving to another browser or device.

Use **Ctrl+Shift+S** to export and **Ctrl+Shift+O** to import. On touch screens, both actions are in **Guide**.
