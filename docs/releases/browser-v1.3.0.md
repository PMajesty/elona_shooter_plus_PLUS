# Browser release 1.3.0

- **Aim Assist** is an optional Game MOD for touch and mouse, available in both editions. It nudges near misses toward an enemy or Sister by at most four game pixels. It starts off and stays with the adventure save.
- **Sound settings** have separate Master, Effects and Ambience sliders. Open Sound on the title screen or Menu / Pause during play, including the tavern. Lowering Ambience quiets the forest and town backgrounds while keeping weapons and other effects audible.
- The new controls use the game's artwork and have English and Russian labels. Sound preferences are remembered separately from adventure saves.
- The mouse reticle and firing point now share fractional pointer coordinates when the game is scaled.

Existing adventures remain compatible with save v46. They keep Aim Assist off.
Weapon spread, damage, experience and companion orders are unchanged by this MOD.

Refresh the [game](https://pastalapizza.site/elona/) to load the update.
